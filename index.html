Topology-Aware Portfolio Optimization

Persistent market topology, Wasserstein and MMD asset geometry, distribution-aware clustering, and causal regime-aware allocation.

Implemented research pipeline · empirical validation in progress

Research question

Does persistent market topology plus distribution-aware asset selection produce more stable out-of-sample portfolios than conventional portfolio optimization?

Pipeline

  1. Adjusted prices
  2. Causal log returns
  3. Persistent homology
  4. Wasserstein + MMD
  5. K-medoids selection
  6. Regime-aware weights
  7. Walk-forward test

Method structure

Time geometry

Rolling market-state point clouds are analyzed with Vietoris-Rips first persistent homology. Short-lived features are filtered, while surviving persistence forms a regime score.

Asset geometry

Trailing empirical return distributions are compared through Wasserstein distance and maximum mean discrepancy.

Portfolio construction

K-medoids selects representatives. A constrained optimizer adjusts risk aversion, covariance shrinkage, and weight caps using the causal regime score.

Evaluation

A strict walk-forward engine deducts transaction costs and compares the full model with all-asset MVO, equal weighting, and SPY.
